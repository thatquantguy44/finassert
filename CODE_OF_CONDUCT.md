# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good faith, give actionable technical feedback, accept
responsibility for mistakes, and keep financial or personal data out of public
discussions. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention,
and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions or ban participants whose behavior
is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Report conduct concerns
privately through the repository's maintainer contact or GitHub reporting tools.
Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
